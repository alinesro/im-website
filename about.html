About Inner Meaning Wellbeing

Inner Meaning Wellbeing exists to make room for what sits beneath the story a person tells first — the argument, the job, the move. Underneath that lives something older: how the person learned to read themselves, and what they decided made them who they are. Events don't arrive raw, they arrive already interpreted; two people lose the same job, and one feels a door closing while the other hears confirmation of something they always suspected about themselves. The difference isn't in the event, but in the meaning attached to it — usually built early on, from experiences the person was too young to question. Meanings built early can be revisited. That's the premise of the work here.

The person behind the counsellor

I'm Aline, 30 years old, born and raised in Brazil, living in Sydney since 2023. I'm passionate about philosophy, and art is my favourite hobby. On weekends I try to step away from screens and reconnect with myself and the people I love. I've been in therapy myself for over ten years — that's where I learned to look at myself with more lightness, compassion, and care, and that's what I bring to my clients.

My professional background

My background started in Psychology, followed by postgraduate training in Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) and Schema Therapy, both completed in Brazil. Here in Australia, I work as a registered counsellor with the Australian Counselling Association (Member 87029). I work with adults, in both English and Portuguese. What comes up most often in this room is anxiety, excessive worry and ruminating thoughts, self-criticism, self-esteem and perfectionism, relationships, boundaries and emotional dependency, and life transitions — including the particular weight of building a life far from where it all began.

How I work

The foundation of my work is Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), which teaches that while we can't control everything, we can work with our thoughts and how we respond to situations. Through its techniques, we learn to make our thinking more flexible and to regulate our emotions. It's considered a gold-standard approach for many concerns, grounded in evidence-based practice. Schema Therapy is also part of the work, since it looks further back, at the patterns formed early in life that still shape how a person sees themselves and what they expect from others.

What you can expect from our work together

The goal of my work is to help you revisit the meanings you carry and build a steadier, kinder relationship with yourself. I work on social skills and assertive communication, help regulate emotions, build self-respect and self-care, and address what comes with moving to a new country — starting over professionally, being far from family, and finding your place between two cultures.
