24/7 Crisis and Mental Health Support

If you or someone you know needs urgent help, please reach
out to one of the 24/7 services below, or call 000 right away.

Crisis & suicide support

Lifeline

24/7 crisis support and suicide prevention.

13 11 14

Suicide Call Back Service

24/7 counselling for people feeling suicidal, worried about someone, or affected by suicide.

1300 659 467

NSW Mental Health Line

24/7 mental health advice, assessment and referral to NSW Health mental health services.

1800 011 511

Beyond Blue

24/7 mental health support for anxiety, depression and emotional distress.

1300 22 4636

Children & young people

Kids Helpline

24/7 counselling for children and young people aged 5–25.

1800 55 1800

Child Protection Helpline

24/7 line to report a child or young person at risk of harm.

13 21 11

Domestic, family & sexual violence

1800RESPECT

24/7 counselling and support for people impacted by domestic, family or sexual violence.

1800 737 732

NSW Domestic Violence Line

24/7 crisis counselling, advocacy and referrals for women, with or without children, experiencing domestic or family violence.

1800 656 463

NSW Rape Crisis

Statewide phone and online crisis counselling for anyone impacted by sexual violence, including trauma counselling for adults sexually assaulted as children.

1800 424 017

Alcohol & other drugs

Alcohol & Drug Information Service (ADIS)

24/7 advice, information, counselling and referrals for those affected by alcohol or other drug use.

1800 250 015
