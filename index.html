It's normal to feel unsure before a first session. You might wonder what to say, whether you'll get emotional, or if this is even the right step. Therapy is simply a conversation with a purpose. Together, we look at what's been happening, make sense of the patterns behind it, and work toward change that actually lasts. Some parts will feel harder than others, you'll never be pushed to share more than you're ready for. We move at your pace.
